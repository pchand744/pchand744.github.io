How to read this simulation

Letters identify vertices for the reader. The agents work on anonymous vertices with locally numbered ports. The drawing uses alphabetical neighbour order to fix one possible port ordering. A dark ring marks the current vertex, dashed lines show a probe or a gathering move, and small badges count agents.

The colouring follows Algorithm 2. The root turns red. Each subsequent vertex is processed on its first DFS visit. Groups of at most ⌈n/3⌉ seekers check all its neighbours. A seeker may wait up to six rounds to meet an oscillating representative. Colour decisions use all graph edges, including edges outside the DFS tree.

In the two-cluster example, the chosen root specifies an illustrative outcome of Algorithm 4. Cluster dispersion, mergers and the external-edge termination test are summarized. Gathering then executes the bottom-up rule in Algorithm 5 before Algorithm 6 invokes rooted colouring. Coverage, oscillations, travel and final settlement are grouped into events. The timeline measures these events; the paper’s O(n) round bound and O(log n) agent memory bound describe the distributed protocol.
