About me

My current research interests lie in distributed graph algorithms, mobile-agent computation, and fault tolerance. I study how agents with limited memory and local information coordinate to explore networks and solve graph problems, with an emphasis on time and memory efficiency and reliable computation in the presence of faults.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, under the supervision of Prof. Anisur Rahaman Molla. I hold an M.Sc. in Pure Mathematics from the University of Burdwan and a B.Sc. in Mathematics from Bankura Christian College.

02 /

Research

I.

Distributed graph computation

Computing spanning trees, dominating sets, and graph statistics through local coordination, with an emphasis on round complexity and memory efficiency.

II.

Mobile agents & robots

Exploration and computation in anonymous networks, where mobile agents move through a graph and exchange information through local interactions.

III.

Fault tolerance

Designing resilient coordination and exploration algorithms in the presence of Byzantine agents and hazardous network nodes.

IV.

Network structure

Agent-based algorithms for triangle counting, truss decomposition, triangle centrality, clustering coefficients, and butterfly analysis.
